ESP32-WROOM-32 devKit -It's a microprocessor with integrated Wi-Fi and dual-mode Bluetooth .
  
SSD1306 OLED display - Used as an interface between sensors and user.
  
DS18B20 - temperature sensor.
  
TDS sensor - Measures Total Dissolved Solids; the sensor voltage is converted into an estimated ppm value by the firmware.

Turbidity sensor - Measures the amount of cloudiness in the solution.

HX711 amplifier - Interfaces the load cell with ESP32 to measure weight.

5 kg load cell - I have taken 5Kg weight as threshold weight.

Breadboard and jumper wires - Used for connections and placement of sensors.
